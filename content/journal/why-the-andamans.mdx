There is a version of this company that launches everywhere at once. A directory,
a search box, a long list of destinations, and almost nothing behind any of them.
It would look bigger on day one and be worse in every way that matters.

We are doing the opposite. Yuvoy opens in one market — Havelock, Neil and Port
Blair — and stays there for a full season before going anywhere else.

## The gap is unusually obvious here

The Andamans are a high-intent, high-value destination. People plan trips here
months ahead, fly a long way, and arrive with a clear idea of wanting to be _in_
the water rather than beside it.

And then almost nothing about the actual days is arrangeable before they land.

The dives, the boats, the kitchens and the walks are real, and many of them are
run by people who have been doing it for years. What is missing is any way to
find them, understand what a day involves, and commit to one — before you are
standing at a counter asking.

That is not a marketing problem. It is a plumbing problem, and it is the one we
are building for.

## A season is a natural clock

The islands have a rhythm that most destinations do not. The monsoon closes much
of the water, and the boats come back with the season. That gives us something
rare for a young company: a deadline set by the place rather than by us.

We build while the boats are idle. We open when they return.

## Small enough to do properly

Three islands is a scope one team can actually cover. We can meet operators in
person, film with them rather than briefing them from a distance, and understand
what a day genuinely asks of a guest before we describe it to anyone.

That matters more than it sounds. Nearly every claim we would like to make about
an experience — how hard it is, what it includes, who it suits — is only
trustworthy if someone has been there.

## What this means for what you see

It means the site is smaller than it could be. There are no listings yet, no
prices, and nothing to book. We would rather show you four honest categories than
four hundred invented entries.

It also means that when the first experiences do appear, they will be ones we
have seen, with operators we have met, described in terms we can stand behind.

One destination, done properly. Then everywhere worth going.
