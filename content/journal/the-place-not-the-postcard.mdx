Search the Andamans and you'll find the same handful of images: white sand, turquoise shallows, a hammock strung between two palms. All true. All also a kind of erasure — a place emptied of the people who live it, so it can be sold as a place to escape to.

## What the postcard leaves out

The tide tables the fishermen read like a newspaper. The forest that hides birds found nowhere else on earth. The market before dawn. The dive crew who have known these reefs for years and still talk about them like a first date.

None of it photographs as easily as the hammock. All of it is the actual place.

> Honest and rooted — never a brochure version.

## Rooted, on purpose

We are not a travel company that discovered the Andamans. We operate here. Our stories come from a place we work in every day, not one we flew into for a shoot.

That's the difference we're chasing: to be more rooted and real than the global brands, and more considered than the local listings — to show the place as it actually is, and let that be enough.
